Operator notes

Account controlsForce sign-out when a device is lost or a session needs resetting. Deleting an account also removes its synced viewing data.
IP blocks persistBlocked addresses are stored on disk and survive restarts. Unblock from the chips under the stream table.
Idle is not goneA stream reads Idle once its player stops polling (~30s) and disappears after two minutes of silence. Paused streams stay listed until resumed or stopped.
